Connect Jira

Three things and QA Report can file bugs to your Jira Cloud site.

  1. Your Jira site

    The address you open Jira at.

  2. Your account

    Create a token on your Atlassian account page, then paste it here.

  3. Check and save

    QA Report signs in once to make sure the three values work.

Share with your team

Export the labels and the report template to a file and send it to your team. Each person imports it here, then connects with their own token. The file holds no site, email or token.

Your token stays in this browser profile and goes only to your Jira site. QA Report has no server and collects nothing.
